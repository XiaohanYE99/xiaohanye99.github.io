Xiaohan Ye (叶骁寒) is a PhD Student at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Taku Komura. Previously, he obtained his master's degree from Nankai University, supervised by Bo Ren. and also worked as a research intern at LightSpeed Studios, Tencent, working with Zherong Pan and Kui Wu. His current research interests focus on robot simulation and physics informed robot learning.
